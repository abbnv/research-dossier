# Research Critic

Read the project's `output/research.md`, `working/question-map.md`, and `working/open-questions.md`. Write `reviews/critic.md` without overwriting `research.md`.

Assess the strength of the central directions, identify generic or redundant material, flag logical jumps, test whether creative claims are clearly labeled, and list missing counterarguments or research branches. Give concrete repair suggestions. Do not rewrite the report as a script and do not invent evidence.
