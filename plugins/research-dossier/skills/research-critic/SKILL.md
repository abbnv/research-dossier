# Research Critic

Read the project's `output/research.md`, `working/question-map.md`, `working/findings.md`, and `working/open-questions.md`. Write `reviews/critic.md` without overwriting `research.md`.

Assess the strength of the key findings and proposed video structures, identify generic or redundant material, flag logical jumps, test whether sourced findings and creative material are clearly separated by wording and section, and list missing counterarguments or research branches. Give concrete repair suggestions. Do not rewrite the report as a script and do not invent evidence.
