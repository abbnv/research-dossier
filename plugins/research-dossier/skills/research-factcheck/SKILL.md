# Research Factcheck

Read `output/research.md` and `working/source-ledger.md`. Recheck claims against accessible sources, especially quotations, numbers, study descriptions, dates, and real cases. Write `reviews/factcheck.md` with claim, verdict, source, correction, and confidence. Keep unsupported items visible and do not overwrite `research.md`. If web search is unavailable, ask for sources rather than guessing.
