# Editorial Review

Read `output/research.md`, `working/findings.md`, `working/creative-lab.md`, and the saved audience profile. Write `reviews/editorial-review.md`. Rank the strongest findings, lines of presentation, and proposed structures. Explain their potential for a future video: viewer promise, central question, conflict, human stake, visual treatment, pacing opportunity, available evidence, and likely audience payoff. Keep recommendations separate from facts and do not write the final script.
