# Reference case: «Иммунитет к изменениям»

Use this topic as the repository's end-to-end example. The expected dossier should compare the Immunity to Change framework with adjacent ideas such as competing commitments, self-sabotage, procrastination, cognitive dissonance, status quo bias, loss aversion, and implementation intentions.

The example must visibly separate:

- what the authors of the framework claim;
- what research supports or complicates it;
- what is an interpretation;
- what is a creative hypothesis;
- what is an invented short illustration.

It must contain evidence, limitations, real cases, verified or paraphrased quotes with a Russian translation plus original wording, media references, metaphors, the full ten wild concepts in the final `research.md`, and a source ledger. It remains a research dossier, not a finished screenplay.
