# Сценарный синтез

Run this after the broad research pass, not as a gate before it. The purpose is to show how the collected material could become a future video without drafting the final script.

## Short findings first

Write 7–10 bullets that answer «что мы узнали?» in plain Russian. Then expand each finding with evidence, meaning, limitations, and scenario value.

## Recommended structure

Offer one strongest structure with:

- viewer promise;
- opening problem or recognizable situation;
- unexpected explanation;
- evidence and concepts;
- human conflict or case;
- turn, contradiction, or limitation;
- practical implication;
- ending question or takeaway;
- facts, quotes, visuals, and gaps available for each beat.

## Alternative structures

Offer 2–4 viable alternatives, such as:

- a personal or documented story;
- a popular myth being dismantled;
- a comparison of competing explanations;
- a practical experiment;
- one extended metaphor or visual system.

For every structure explain its promise, strongest materials, weak point, risk of overreach, and what additional evidence would improve it.

## Possible lines of presentation

Generate a broad list of ways to frame the theme. A line may be a paradox, practical promise, human conflict, neighboring concept, visual metaphor, or unusual protagonist. Preserve strong and surprising lines even when they are not used in the recommended structure.

## No invention by synthesis

Do not create a case, quote, result, or historical event to make a structure look complete. If a beat needs material that was not found, write «нужно найти» in `open-questions.md` and expose the gap in the final dossier.
